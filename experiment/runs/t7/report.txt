ROOT CAUSES:
1. Clutter props (Rubble, Fungus, Bones, Puddle) were buried in the floor. spawner.gd hardcoded `FLOOR_Y := 0.0`, but the GridMap floor is not at y=0. GridMap cells are centred in their 2 m cell, so cell y=0 has its centre at y=1.0 and the 0.1 m slab top is at y=1.05. The props' bottoms landed at y=0.0, 1.05 m under the surface, and the opaque slab hid them. Measured before: Rubble bottom=0.000 against a surface at 1.050.
2. Skull, Candle and Bucket were all stacked at x=0, z=0 (an error was logged per prop, 6 times in the headless run). `_drop_small_props` ran `prop.global_position.y = ...` before `decor.add_child(prop)`. Reading global_position on a node outside the tree fails with `Condition "!is_inside_tree()" is true. Returning: Transform3D()` and yields Vector3.ZERO. The setter then wrote (0, y, 0) back, so the intended x/z were lost. The three props sat at (0, ~1.2, 0), overlapping each other. In the before screenshot only the Bucket is visible. The Skull and Candle are hidden inside it.
3. Lantern and Chest were placed behind the camera. `_place_ahead_of_player` offset along `+cam.global_basis.z`. A Camera3D looks down its local -Z, so +Z is backward. They ended up at z≈11.5 and z≈10.6 against a camera at z=7. Both were `is_position_behind` = true, `inFrustum` = false.
