ROOT CAUSES:
1. Hard-coded ground height. `const FLOOR_Y := 0.0` in spawner.gd assumed the floor top is at world y=0. The floor is a GridMap with 2 m cells and the default cell_center_y, so the slab centre is at y=1.0 and its top is at y=1.05 (`surface_y()` returns 1.05). The four `_scatter_clutter` props (Rubble, Fungus, Bones, Puddle) were placed at FLOOR_Y + half height (y of about 0.01 to 0.2), so they were buried about 1 m under the slab. The probe showed their bottoms at y=0.000 against a floor top of 1.05.
2. `global_position` set before `add_child`. In `_drop_small_props`, `prop.global_position.y = ...` runs while the prop is not in the tree. `get_global_transform()` fails with `!is_inside_tree()` and returns identity, so the setter wrote (0, y, 0). Skull, Candle and Bucket all landed stacked at x=0, z=0 (at the camera's look-at line, one inside another) instead of at their intended spots. This produced 3 ERROR lines, one per prop, in the headless run.
3. Wrong sign on the camera forward vector. `_place_ahead_of_player` used `+ cam.global_basis.z`. In Godot a camera looks along -Z, so Lantern and Chest were placed behind the camera (z=11.5 and 10.6, while the camera is at z=7). The probe reported `is_position_behind=true` for both.
