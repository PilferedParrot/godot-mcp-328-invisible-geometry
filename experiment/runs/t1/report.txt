ROOT CAUSES:
1. Wrong floor height. spawner.gd used a hard-coded `FLOOR_Y := 0.0` for Rubble, Fungus, Bones and Puddle. The GridMap floor cells are 2 m cells with the 0.1 m slab centred vertically, so the real top surface is at y=1.05 (floor underside y=0.95). Those four props sat at y 0 to 0.4, below the floor, and were buried in it. The audit reported BURIED for all four.
2. Transform written before add_child(). In `_drop_small_props` (Skull, Candle, Bucket), `prop.global_position.y = ...` ran before `decor.add_child(prop)`. The game log showed `Condition "!is_inside_tree()" is true` at `get_global_transform`, with a backtrace into spawner.gd:31. Because the node was not in the tree, the x/z set in `position` was lost. All three ended up stacked at world x=0, z=0, which the audit reported as ORIGIN. They were not on their intended spots.
3. Wrong direction vector. `_place_ahead_of_player` (Lantern, Chest) offset along `cam.global_basis.z`. In Godot a camera looks down -Z, so +Z is behind it. Both props were placed behind the camera and the audit reported NOT_IN_FRUSTUM (near plane), so they were never in view.
