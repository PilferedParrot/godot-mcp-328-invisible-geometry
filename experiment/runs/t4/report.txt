ROOT CAUSES:
1. Four props (Rubble, Fungus, Bones, Puddle) were buried inside the floor. `_scatter_clutter` placed them at the hard-coded `FLOOR_Y = 0.0`. The GridMap centres each slab in its 2 m cell (`cell_center_y` is true by default), so the slab spans y 0.95 to 1.05 and its top is at 1.05, not 0. Before the fix their bottoms were at y = 0.0, so each was below the slab and hidden.
2. Three props (Skull, Candle, Bucket) all collapsed to the world origin, so they were stacked together and mostly hidden. `_drop_small_props` set `prop.global_position.y` before `decor.add_child(prop)`. A node outside the tree can't compute its global transform, so each call logged `Condition "!is_inside_tree()" is true` and read back an identity transform. That reset x and z to 0. The log showed one such error per prop, plus more from the later runs of the same line. After the fix, the log has no error lines.
3. Two props (Lantern, Chest) were placed behind the camera. `_place_ahead_of_player` added `cam.global_basis.z * distance`. In Godot a camera looks down -Z, so +basis.z points backward. They landed at z ≈ 10.6 to 11.5, behind the camera at z = 7, and `is_position_behind` returned true for both.
