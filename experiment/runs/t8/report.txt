ROOT CAUSES:
1. Props in `_scatter_clutter` (Rubble, Fungus, Bones, Puddle) were placed from a hard-coded `FLOOR_Y := 0.0`. The GridMap floor is not at y=0. The cell is centred on y (cell_center_y default true), so the 0.1 m slab spans y 0.95..1.05 and its top is at 1.05. The four props sat at y 0..0.4, entirely under the floor, so the floor hid them. The audit reported BURIED for all four.
2. `_drop_small_props` (Skull, Candle, Bucket) set `prop.global_position.y` before `decor.add_child(prop)`. Reading global_transform on a node outside the tree fails with `Condition "!is_inside_tree()"` and returns an identity transform. The setter then wrote the y into that identity transform, which threw away the x/z set one line earlier. All three props ended up at world x=0, z=0 (audit: ORIGIN), stacked in one spot instead of at their intended positions. They were visible but misplaced. The console logged the is_inside_tree error 6 times.
3. `_place_ahead_of_player` (Lantern, Chest) added `cam.global_basis.z * distance`. A Camera3D looks along -Z, so +Z is behind the camera. The props were placed 4-5 m behind the player and fell outside the near plane (audit: NOT_IN_FRUSTUM).
